# Competitor analysis: digital invitations and RSVP (24 Oct 2026)

Compared: WooowInvites, Pressed Love, Joy (withjoy.com), AreOne, Cardia Studios, VowPost, against AisleFlow2 as of commit 8eadb13 plus the uncommitted meal-options work.

Sources are each site's own marketing pages, read on 24 Oct 2026. Features are as each vendor describes them; none was tried in the product. "–" means the pages I read didn't mention it, not that it doesn't exist.

## 1. Where we stand

**Ahead of most:** we actually send messages. Every competitor except Joy has couples copy and paste a link into WhatsApp themselves. We have:
- email and SMS campaigns with templates, scheduling and drafts;
- open and click tracking;
- replying YES or NO by text;
- household RSVP with per-event attendance;
- custom questions;
- a registry with cash funds and payout handles (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, PayPal);
- guest photo uploads;
- a QR code per guest;
- the publish checklist;
- an owner Design Studio.

**Behind on the "wow" layer** that every one-off-price competitor leads with:
- music when the invitation opens;
- a save-the-date that turns into the full invitation;
- a QR photo album with guestbook and voice notes;
- a seating chart;
- invitations in other languages;
- share video or share images.

**Behind on host tools:** guest limits per party, co-hosts, an address collector, and a caterer export.

**Pricing:** everyone except Joy lets couples build and preview for free and pay once to publish ($49 to $200). Our plan currently makes Essentials free to publish (decision 2 in the billing plan). The market says to charge at publish.

## 4. Gaps worth closing (table stakes)

Most paying competitors have these, and couples will expect them.

1. **Music on open.** A curated track or the couple's own upload. It plays only after the guest taps to open, which browsers require anyway. Effort: S.
2. **One-tap sharing.** WhatsApp (`wa.me` link with the guest's personal URL), copy link, and the system share sheet on every guest row and in bulk. No WhatsApp API needed. Effort: S.
3. **Plus-one and party-size limits per guest** instead of yes/no. Effort: S.
4. **Caterer export.** A CSV of meals, dietary tags and plus-one meals, using the meal counts just added. Effort: S.
5. **RSVP lock and auto-close.** Close at the deadline automatically, or let the couple lock replies by hand. Effort: S.
6. **Page-view tracking per guest link.** "Opened" for link shares, not just emails. The tracking routes already exist. Effort: S.
7. **Save-the-date mode.** The same link shows a short save-the-date page until the couple switches it to the full invitation. Effort: M.
8. **Free build and preview, pay to publish.** Ties into Publish / Go live, which is already built. Effort: S on top of the billing plan.

## 6. Suggested tiers (updates the billing plan)

The market suggests three paid levels rather than free plus Premium:

- **Free:** build and preview everything, no publishing. Matches every competitor.
- **Essentials (~$59–79 once):** publish, unlimited guests, email sending and reminders, RSVP with meals and questions, registry, photo uploads, music (curated), WhatsApp sharing.
- **Premium (~$129–149 once):** adds SMS, the Design Studio, your own song, save-the-date, Memories (guestbook, voice notes, slideshow), seating chart, a second language and a custom URL.
- **Add-ons:** done-for-you design ($299+), extra languages, and Memories on its own for Essentials couples.

The suggested prices sit between VowPost/AreOne ($49–99) and Pressed Love (€175–395). The case for charging more than VowPost is that we actually send email and SMS, which no one-off competitor does. The prices are my suggestion, not market data.

## 7. Suggested order

1. Fix the RSVP bug that lets one guest link overwrite other guests' replies. It's a security issue and goes before anything else.
2. Commit the meal-options work. Then do the table-stakes quick wins from section 4 (music, WhatsApp share, plus-one limits, caterer CSV, RSVP lock, view tracking): about 3–5 days in total.
3. Billing plan Tasks 1–4, updated to the tiers in section 6 (free to build, pay to publish).
4. Memories add-on, then save-the-date mode.
5. Seating chart, then languages, custom URL and the done-for-you service.

Effort sizes are my estimates: S is under a day, M is 1–3 days, L is a week or more.
